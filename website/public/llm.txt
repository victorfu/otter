# Otter

Official website: https://victorfu.github.io/otter/
Project repository: https://github.com/victorfu/otter

Otter is a local-first transcription app for audio and video.

Key pages:
- Home: https://victorfu.github.io/otter/
- Documentation: https://victorfu.github.io/otter/docs
- Privacy Policy: https://victorfu.github.io/otter/docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md

Key capabilities:
- Audio and video transcription
- URL transcription
- Speaker diarization
- AI assistant summaries
- Multiple export formats
- Multilingual support
- Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux
