## New

- 🎧 **Transcribe files** — drop in audio or video, or a whole folder, and get a transcript you can edit, search and export
- 🎙️ **Record and transcribe** — record a consultation or a meeting and get a transcript with speaker labels
- ⌨️ **Dictation** — press a shortcut, speak, and Otter types into the app you are using
- 💻 **Runs on your computer** — models download once and transcribe on your device; with Whisper models, a list of expected words keeps names and terms spelled your way
